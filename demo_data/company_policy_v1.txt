# Company Policy v1.0 (2022)

## 4.2 Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Submit the flexible work form before approval.

## 5.1 Leave
Employees must notify Human Resources before taking extended leave.

## 7 Records
Records must be retained for 7 years. See Section 9.4 for exceptions.

## 8 Employee Identification
Every employee must include their Employee ID in leave requests.

## 10 Conduct
Employees must report an incident as soon as possible to the appropriate approval contact.

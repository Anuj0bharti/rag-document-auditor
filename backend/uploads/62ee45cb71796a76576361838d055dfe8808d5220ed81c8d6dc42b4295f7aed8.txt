# Company Policy v2.0 (2026)

## 4.2 Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval.

## 5.1 Leave
Staff members are required to inform HR before taking prolonged leave.

## 7 Records
Records must be retained for 7 years. The older 2022 policy should be consulted for legacy exceptions.

## 8 Employee Identification
Every staff member must include their Staff ID in leave requests.

## 10 Conduct
Employees must report an incident within 24 hours to the Compliance Manager.
